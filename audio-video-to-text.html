Frequently Asked Questions

Is my audio or video file uploaded to a server?

No. The speech-recognition model is downloaded once to your browser and all transcription happens locally on your device using your browser's own processing power. Your media file itself is never sent anywhere.

What file types are supported?

Most common audio formats (MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG) and video formats (MP4, MOV, WEBM, MKV) that your browser can decode.

Why does the first transcription take longer?

The first time you use the tool, your browser downloads the speech-recognition model (a one-time download, cached afterwards). After that, transcriptions start much faster.

Is there a file length or size limit?

There's no hard limit, but longer files take proportionally longer to process since everything runs on your device. Clips under 25 minutes transcribe quickly on most modern computers and phones.
